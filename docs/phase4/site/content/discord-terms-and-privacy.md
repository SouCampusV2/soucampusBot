## Страница `/terms/discord` — «Ordering through Discord»

**Ordering through Discord**

These terms explain how custom build orders work on the SouCampus Discord
server. They add to our general [Terms of Service](/terms); where they differ,
the general Terms win for anything about payment, licensing and refunds.

### 1. Opening a ticket
- Use the ticket panel in our Discord server and choose **Order a build**. A
  private channel opens that only you and the SouCampus team can see.
- You can have more than one ticket, but you can open at most one new ticket
  per minute.

### 2. Your order form
- Answer the order form: what you need, deadline, map size, theme, Minecraft
  version and budget. References and extra details are optional.
- You get a **price guide** based on the map size — about €1.5 per block of map
  side (for example, 150×150 ≈ €225). It is a guide, not a price: the final
  price is the quote you accept.
- Nothing is shown to builders until you press **Submit order**. Until then
  you can change anything.

### 3. Quotes
- After you submit, builders of the SouCampus team see your order **and your
  Discord name**, and can send you quotes (price, deadline, message) or ask
  questions.
- You can accept one quote, decline any quote with a reason, or start a private
  discussion with a builder.
- You can edit your order until you accept a quote. After that, changes are
  agreed with your builder or a manager — the builder priced what they saw.

### 4. Payment and work
- Payment is confirmed **manually** by a SouCampus manager. Work starts after
  the payment is confirmed. [Payment methods: …]
- Deadlines are agreed per order. A manager may extend a deadline; you will be
  told in your ticket.
- [Revisions: how many rounds of changes are included …]

### 5. Delivery and review
- When the work is delivered, the order is marked complete and you are asked
  for a review (1–5 stars and a comment). One review per order.
- We may ask whether we can show your build in our portfolio and your review
  on our website. **Nothing is published without your yes**, and we never show
  your name next to a portfolio build.

### 6. Cancelling and closing
- You can cancel an order yourself **before** you accept a quote — free of
  charge.
- After you accept a quote, cancelling goes through a manager. Refunds follow
  the [refund policy](/terms#refunds).
- Orders left in the form stage for 3 days are closed automatically (you get a
  reminder after 2 days). You can always open a new ticket.

### 7. When a ticket closes
- The channel becomes read-only and is **deleted 48 hours later**, unless a
  manager keeps it archived.
- A **transcript** (a text file of the whole ticket conversation) is sent to
  you by Discord DM and kept by the SouCampus team. See
  [Privacy → Discord](/privacy#discord).

### 8. Team applications
- Builders can apply through **Join the team**. A manager accepts or rejects
  the application and tells you why if it is rejected.

### 9. Conduct
- Be respectful in tickets. We may close tickets that are abusive or spam.

## Раздел `/privacy#discord` — «Our Discord bot»

**Our Discord bot (BoCampus)**

When you use tickets on our Discord server, our bot processes:

- **Who you are on Discord:** your Discord user ID and display name.
- **What you send in tickets:** your order form answers, quotes you receive,
  messages and files in your ticket channel, your review.
- **Order history:** stages of your order (submitted, accepted, paid,
  delivered, closed) and dates.

**Why:** to run your order — show it to builders, keep the conversation,
confirm payment and delivery, and resolve disputes.

**Where and how long:**
- The bot runs on our own server [in the EU — Hostinger, Lithuania? check].
  Its database keeps order records **for as long as we need them for the
  order and our accounting** [period: …]; database backups are kept for
  14 days.
- Ticket channels are deleted 48 hours after a ticket closes, unless a manager
  keeps them. Before deletion, a **transcript** of the conversation is sent to
  you and to a staff-only channel on our Discord server.
- Discord itself stores messages under the
  [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

**On our website** your build and review appear only if you said yes in your
ticket, and builds are shown without your name. Reviews are signed with your
Discord display name.

**Your rights:** to get a copy of your data or ask us to delete it, open a
Support ticket or write to [support email]. We will remove what we are not
required to keep.
